Where are RDR2 Camera Photos Saved?

Your camera snapshots are stored in your Windows Rockstar Games profile directory as files starting with PRDR3.

%USERPROFILE%\Documents\Rockstar Games\Red Dead Redemption 2\Profiles\<Profile-ID>\
1
Press Win + R on your keyboard to open the Windows Run prompt.
2
Paste the copied path above and press Enter to open your profile folder.
3
Drag and drop all files starting with PRDR3 directly into this web tool.
